About Jack
Born Here.
Know Every Road.

I'm Jack Arland, a local from Kuta Lombok. I've been in the scooter rental business for over 10 years — helping surfers, backpackers, and travellers explore this island safely and freely.

When you rent from me, you're not just getting a bike — you're getting a local friend who knows every road, every surf break, and every hidden gem on this island. Ask me anything.

I started this business to support my family here in Kuta Lombok, and to give travellers a genuinely great local experience. I love meeting new people and making sure your time here is unforgettable.

I speak Indonesian and English, and I'm always available on WhatsApp — day or night. Come find me near Biru Inn & Café in the heart of Kuta, or just message me and I'll come to you.
